Contact🌍 Public page

How to reach the studio, and a form that actually delivers. Shows the studio's phone, WhatsApp, email, address and teaching hours in the visitor's own time zone, a map link, the response time to expect, and a message form that writes into the studio's inbox so nothing depends on an email client being configured. What you can do here: Send the message. Open to anyone, no sign-in needed. It is deliberately indexed by search engines so families can find the studio.

Who it is for. Anyone. No sign-in needed.

Why it matters. For a tutoring studio the contact page is a sales page. A parent who cannot see a phone number, cannot tell whether you are open, and gets no acknowledgement after writing, contacts the next tutor instead.

How to use this page
  1. Use the WhatsApp or call button for anything urgent — both are one tap on a phone.
  2. Check the teaching hours: they are shown converted into your own time zone, not the studio's.
  3. Use the form for anything that needs a written record. You get a reference number back.
  4. Choose the right subject on the form — it routes the message to the right person and sets the response time you are promised.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access, and can edit the content behind it in Settings.
  • Tutor: Full access — it is a public page.
  • Parent: Full access — it is a public page.
  • Learner: Full access — it is a public page.
