Scheme of work

Scheme of work🎓 Staff only

At the start of a term enter every subject topic. Follow coverage, evaluate each learner on each topic, push scores into the scoresheet. What you can do here: Add topic, Create term. Requires sign-in as a tutor, admin or owner. Families cannot open it and the database refuses their queries even if they try the URL directly.

Who it is for. Tutors author the content, learners work through it, parents watch the progress.

Why it matters. This is the actual teaching product. Everything a parent judges you on — scores, mastery, homework — originates from what is planned here.

How to use this page
  1. Pick the engagement, and the learner if it is a group.
  2. Add or import the items you need — topics, tasks, questions or reading links.
  3. Publish, so the learner sees it on their own dashboard.
  4. Track completion and scores as the work comes back in.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: No access.
  • Learner: No access.
