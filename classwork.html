ℹ️ What is this page?
Classwork🧑🏾‍🏫 Tutors post · families follow

The class feed: assignments, quizzes, materials and comment-only returns, organised by topic. Staff post items here; the class’s students and their parents see them instantly on their own portals. Row Level Security enforces who sees what in the database — parents only ever see their own children’s classes, a learner only their own.

Who it is for. Tutors post and edit the work; learners open the materials and hand in; parents follow the thread of what was set.

Why it matters. This is the teaching product in motion — every item here is a promise the class can see, with its due date and its points on record.

How to use this page
  1. Staff: fill the “Post new classwork” card — pick the class, a topic, the kind (assignment, quiz, material or question) and a clear title.
  2. Set a due date so it lands on the to-do bar of every student in that class. Leave Points blank for a comment-only return; give points when it will be scored.
  3. Paste a resource link (Drive / YouTube / any https URL) — files are linked, never uploaded, so the free database never fills.
  4. Click ✅ Post classwork. It appears for the class immediately — no publish step needed.
  5. Change your mind? Edit fixes a typo, Duplicate re-posts it for next week, Delete removes it. Families only ever read.
  6. Use the chips and filters above the list to slice by class, kind or search — then Export CSV / PDF for records or a parents’ evening.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Post, edit and delete across every class.
  • Tutor: Post, edit and delete for the classes assigned to them.
  • Parent: Read-only, and only for their own children’s classes.
  • Learner: Read-only, and only for their own classes.
