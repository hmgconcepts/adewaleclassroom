Payment plans🎓 Staff only

Splits a large fee into an instalment schedule and tracks every part of it. You enter the total, the number of instalments and how often they fall due; every date and amount is generated for you. What you can do here: Generate schedule. Requires sign-in as a tutor, admin or owner. Families cannot open it and the database refuses their queries even if they try the URL directly.

Who it is for. Owner, admin and staff. A parent sees their own plan in the family portal, read-only.

Why it matters. Termly fees are routinely paid in parts, and a family who cannot pay ₦180,000 at once will happily pay ₦60,000 three times. Every serious competitor offers instalments; without this the conversation happened in WhatsApp and the tracking lived in somebody's head. The arrears list replaces that.

How to use this page
  1. Choose the family and, optionally, the learner. Both lists auto-fill.
  2. Enter the total owed — not the instalment amount. The split is calculated for you and previewed live as you type.
  3. Choose how many instalments and how often: weekly, fortnightly, monthly or termly.
  4. Press Generate schedule. Every due date and amount is created in one action.
  5. Note that rounding is absorbed by the first instalment, never the last, so a family never meets a surprise odd amount at the end of a plan they had budgeted for.
  6. Press ✓ Mark paid on an instalment as the money arrives; today's date is filled in for you.
  7. Anything past its due date appears in Arrears with the number of days late.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: No access.
  • Learner: No access.
