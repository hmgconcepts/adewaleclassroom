Payment plans

Payment plans💳 Money

What this page is. Splits a large fee into a schedule of instalments and then tracks each one. Enter the total, say how many parts and how often, and the whole schedule is generated for you — you never calculate or type a single due date.

Who it is for. Owner, admin and staff. A parent sees their own plan in the family portal, read-only.

Why it matters. Termly fees in Nigeria are routinely paid in parts, and a family that cannot pay ₦180,000 in one go will happily pay ₦60,000 three times. Every major competitor offers instalments and this studio did not, so the conversation had to happen in WhatsApp and be tracked in somebody's head. The arrears list below replaces that entirely.

How to use this page — step by step
  1. Choose the family. The list fills itself from your parents — nothing to type.
  2. Enter the total the family owes, not the instalment amount. The split is worked out for you.
  3. Choose how many instalments and how often (weekly, fortnightly, monthly or termly).
  4. Press Generate schedule. Every due date and amount is created at once.
  5. Rounding is absorbed by the first instalment, never the last, so a family never gets a surprise odd amount at the end of a plan they had budgeted for.
  6. Mark each instalment paid as the money arrives. Anything past its date appears in Arrears automatically.
