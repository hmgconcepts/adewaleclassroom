Two modes — the paper decides who you are

Registered: only enrolled learners. Enter your student ID (TC-0001). Your official name is loaded and shown — you do not type it to identify yourself (that prevents spelling errors). Open / anonymous: anyone may sit. Registered learners can still enter their ID so the score lands on their scoresheet; guests type a display name only.

Enter the code first. The paper will say whether it is Registered or Open.
