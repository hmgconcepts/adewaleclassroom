Quizzes — Self / Review / Graded👪 Family view

Where every quiz is built and managed. Three kinds: Self-quiz for unmarked practice, Review quiz for finding the gaps after a class, and Graded quiz which pushes straight to the scoresheet. Questions are pasted or imported from CSV — seventeen question families are supported — and each paper carries its own anti-cheat settings. Every saved paper has full lifecycle controls beside it: preview, questions, edit, results, share, close or re-open, archive, duplicate and delete. What you can do here: Parse CSV preview, Save quiz. Requires sign-in. Parents see only their own children; a learner sees only themselves. Row Level Security enforces this in the database, not just in the interface.

Who it is for. Tutors and administrators build papers. A tutor manages only their own. Learners sit papers on the Take a quiz page.

Why it matters. A paper you cannot close keeps collecting sittings after the deadline, and the only previous way to stop it was to delete the paper and lose the results with it. Closing, re-opening and archiving are separate acts for that reason, and closure is enforced by the database rather than merely by the interface.

How to use this page
  1. Fill the header — title, code, kind, access mode and duration.
  2. Import a CSV or paste one. The file is parsed in your browser and never uploaded.
  3. Set the anti-cheat options. Tools a candidate legitimately needs, such as the calculator, are exempt from the violation counter.
  4. Press Parse CSV preview before saving, to see exactly what will be stored.
  5. Manage a saved paper from the buttons beside it: Close stops new sittings without touching the results, Open restarts it, Share produces a link, Results opens the marks, and Archive retires it.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access across every learner.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners assigned to them.
  • Parent: Read-only, and only for their own children.
  • Learner: Read-only, and only their own record.
