📺 Seeing your students while teaching here

While Google Meet / Zoom shares your screen, you have three free ways to keep an eye on students:

  1. Meet picture-in-picture: in Google Meet (Android), tap Home while in the call — Meet shrinks to a floating window showing participants on top of this app. Drag it to a corner. (Zoom: enable Settings → Meeting → Show my video in PiP.)
  2. Split second device: join the Meet/Zoom call muted on your phone and lay it beside the tablet as a dedicated "student monitor".
  3. Classroom Deck hybrid: have students ALSO open your Classroom Deck room link (no audio needed) and share their cameras here — open the 👥 drawer to see the camera grid while Meet handles audio/screen.

Tip: your own camera → tap 📷 Cam. Your face appears in a floating window on this screen, so it is included in the screen share and students see you. Drag it anywhere; tap it to cycle size.

📱 Tablet Live — stream to social (no OBS)

You need exactly 3 things: ① your relay address (the small free web service from relay/no-obs-social-relay/ — deploy once), ② a stream key copied fresh from each platform's live page, ③ this tablet signed in. Miss any one and the ⚠ preflight below tells you which — before a byte leaves the tablet. Full guide with click-paths and troubleshooting: 🌐 Social centre.

Paste just the domain root of the relay you deployed. Example: https://class-relay.yourname.onrender.com. ✅ starts with https:// · ❌ http:// (the browser blocks it), the WHIP path (the app adds /rtc/v1/whip/… itself), or your hosting dashboard URL.
When you deployed the relay you may have set a SECRET environment variable — paste that exact passphrase here. Example: a-long-passphrase-only-you-know. ❌ do NOT paste a platform stream key here: secret = the RELAY's password; stream key = the PLATFORM's password (that goes in a destination row below).
Letters, digits and dashes only; pre-filled as classdeck-<room code>. Example: classdeck-math10a. ❌ spaces or emoji (rejected) — and two teachers streaming at the same time must use different names or the feeds fight.
✅ 16:9 landscape for YouTube / Facebook / Twitch · ✅ 9:16 vertical for TikTok / Instagram (your camera is picture-in-picture beside the tall canvas). ❌ landscape to TikTok = black bars either side — the preflight warns you before you go live.
✅ 15 fps is the recommendation for mobile/hotspot upload · 10 fps when viewers report stutter · 24–30 only on strong broadband. ❌ choosing 30 fps "because it is better" on mobile data usually stutters the stream.
One row per platform. Paste anything in the smart box — a bare stream key, a full rtmp(s)://server/app/KEY URL, or the whole block copied from the platform page — and the platform, server and key are worked out for you. Example key (YouTube): ab3d-1234-5678-9def. ❌ never re-use last week's key on YouTube/Instagram (they expire — the #1 "no preview" cause); copy a fresh one per live session. The server URL fills itself — hand-typing it is the #1 failed-stream cause.
