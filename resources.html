ℹ️ What is this page?
Resource library📚 Staff author · families read

Drive / YouTube / PDF links scoped to an engagement or shared studio-wide. No file uploads into the free database — everything is a link. Assigned students and their parents see their class’s items and the shared shelf on their portal and dashboard work board, read-only (V49 fixed the missing family access that kept these invisible).

Who it is for. Tutors author the content, learners work through it, parents watch the progress.

Why it matters. This is the actual teaching product. Everything a parent judges you on — scores, mastery, homework — originates from what is planned here.

How to use this page
  1. Click Add new and give the resource a title and a Drive / YouTube / PDF link.
  2. Pick the kind so students see the right icon.
  3. Leave Class / group / cohort blank for the shared shelf, or scope the item to one class.
  4. Use Export CSV / Export PDF any time for records or printing.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: Read-only — the class shelves and the shared shelf for their own children (V49).
  • Learner: Read-only — their own classes’ shelves and the shared shelf (V49).
