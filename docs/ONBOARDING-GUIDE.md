# Onboarding — first week in the studio

## If you are the tutor / owner
1. Settings → identity, timezone, currency, cancellation hours.
2. Methodologies → confirm the four seeded methods or write your own.
3. Subjects.
4. Create **yourself** as a Tutor and link your login.
5. Add Learners. Add Parents. Link them (parent–learner).
6. Create one **Engagement** per teaching contract (1:1 *or* group). Add roster seats.
7. Sell a **package** (hour bank) on that engagement.
8. Book the first sessions. Paste a free Jitsi or Meet link.
9. Sit a **diagnostic** so value-added is honest.
10. Show the parent Learner 360 on a call. That is the product.

## If you are a parent
Sign in after approval. You see only your children: calendar, notes that were shared, homework, invoices, reports, Insights for those children. You can request a conference and pay from Payment history.

## If you are a learner
Sign in to see your sessions, homework, practice tests, spaced cards and your own heatmap.

## International
Set timezone on the learner and on the engagement. Use Timezone Desk before offering a slot.
