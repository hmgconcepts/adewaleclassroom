ℹ️ What is this page?
Class stream📣 Staff post · families follow

A Google-Classroom-style feed for each class: announcements, questions and materials, with link previews (Drive, YouTube, any https) — no uploads, ever. Staff post to a class’s stream; that class’s students and their parents see every post on their own portals. Row Level Security enforces who sees what in the database.

Who it is for. Tutors and staff run the feed; learners and parents read their own classes’ streams.

Why it matters. The stream is the class’s front page: whatever is posted here is the first thing a family sees — a clear post prevents ten clarification messages.

How to use this page
  1. Staff: pick the class the post belongs to, the kind (announcement, question or material) and give it a clear title.
  2. Write the body — say exactly what the class should do, and by when.
  3. Set Publish at to schedule a post for later (leave it as-is to post now; families only see posts whose time has arrived).
  4. Paste a media URL (Drive / YouTube / https) for a live preview card — linked, never uploaded.
  5. Click 📣 Post to stream. Use 🗂️ Manage stream posts below to edit, duplicate, delete, export or print anything you have already posted.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Post and manage across every class.
  • Tutor: Post and manage for the classes assigned to them.
  • Parent: Read-only — the streams of their own children’s classes.
  • Learner: Read-only — their own classes’ streams.
