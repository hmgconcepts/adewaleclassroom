Prepaid wallet🎓 Staff only

A prepaid credit account for every family. A parent pays once for a block of lessons or a sum of money, and each attended session is drawn down from that balance automatically. Every movement is a permanent ledger row, so the balance is always the sum of its history and can never quietly drift. What you can do here: Add credit. Requires sign-in as a tutor, admin or owner. Families cannot open it and the database refuses their queries even if they try the URL directly.

Who it is for. Owner, admin and staff can top up and inspect any family's wallet. A signed-in parent sees only their own balance and movements, enforced by row-level security in the database rather than by hiding a button.

Why it matters. Chasing an invoice after the lesson has already been taught is the weakest position a tutoring studio can be in. A prepaid wallet reverses the cash flow — the money arrives first and the teaching draws it down. It is the feature the market leader builds its entire billing around, and it is reported to lift monthly renewals by up to 42% because families top up before they run out instead of being chased afterwards.

How to use this page
  1. Switch the wallet on in Settings and set the low-balance threshold that puts a family on the chase list.
  2. Choose a family from the dropdown — it fills itself from your parents list, so there is nothing to type.
  3. Enter the amount received and the bank reference, then press Add credit.
  4. Pick the unit: Currency holds money, Sessions holds a number of lessons. Studios with one fixed lesson price usually prefer sessions.
  5. Teach as normal. Marking a learner present on the Attendance page deducts the session automatically.
  6. Work the Low balances list weekly — each family has a one-tap WhatsApp link.
  7. To correct a mistake, add a reversing entry rather than editing history. That is what keeps the ledger auditable.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: No access.
  • Learner: No access.
