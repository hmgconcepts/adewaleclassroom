🖥️ CBT homework — auto-fill from CBT results

The blueprint: exams happen once per term, but homework is given many times — and every attempt accumulates. A published graded CBT aimed at a class lands here automatically as a 🖥️ CBT assignment (with a working sit link); physical work you add by hand. Both kinds flow into the same term score sheet.

Modus operandi:

  1. Create a graded CBT on the CBT builder page and publish it to the class — its homework row appears here instantly (do not create CBT rows by hand).
  2. Students press Start from their work board and the result is recorded.
  3. Score the class: press ✍️ Score class on any row below — for CBT assignments the scores auto-fill from each learner's best CBT attempt (scaled to the assignment's maximum — raw 15/20 becomes 7.5/10 when the max is 10); for physical work you type them.
  4. Collate: the 🏅 workbench below turns every scored assignment into one column per learner — physical 📄 and CBT 🖥️ side by side, with totals.
  5. Push: at term's end, 🚀 pushes each learner's cumulative points into the scoresheet (source homework_points).
What each kind is for (unambiguous):
🟢 CBT homework many per term, cumulative → homework workbench
🔵 Graded quiz / mock once per term → scoresheet automatically on submit
🟣 Practice never graded — no scores anywhere

🏆 Why your homework earns points — how it works

Every homework you complete earns points that accumulate for the term. This includes:

  • 📄 Physical assignments — written work, projects, exercises (your teacher scores them by hand)
  • 🖥️ CBT assignments — online homework you take with the Start button; marked automatically, and you can have several per term
  1. Your teacher sets the homework for your class.
  2. You complete it — for CBT, press Start and do it online; your score is saved automatically.
  3. All your scores accumulate: total points / total possible → %.
  4. At the end of the term the cumulative % becomes your continuous-assessment evidence. More homework done = higher it climbs. 💪
