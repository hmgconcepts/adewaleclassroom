Contracts & consent🎓 Staff only

The register of every agreement and consent record between the studio and a family: service agreements (contracts), safeguarding consent, recording / photo consent and custom consents. Each record moves through a clear lifecycle — draft, sent, awaiting signature, signed, void — and a signed record is stamped with who signed and when. A signed record is never deleted (audit integrity); the family can read its own signed copies. What you can do here: Create contract / consent, Send, Record signature, Void, Export. Requires sign-in as staff; families see only signed copies relating to their own children.

Who it is for. The owner and admin draft and sign; tutors may prepare a draft for their own learners.

Why it matters. Consent is a safeguarding and data-protection record. If it is not written down, dated and kept, it did not happen.

How to use this page
  1. Choose contract or consent, give it a title and paste the body.
  2. Attach it to a learner so the family link works.
  3. Move it to sent when the family receives it, then awaiting signature.
  4. When the family signs, open the record and set signed with the signatory name and date — it is now part of the permanent register.
  5. Void a superseded record rather than deleting it.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: Read-only, and only their own signed copies.
  • Learner: No access.
