ℹ️ What is this page?
Digital library📚 Staff author · families read

Catalogued reading and past-paper links with an optional auto-marked comprehension quiz. Items are either shared studio-wide or scoped to one class — assigned students and their parents see them on their portal and dashboard work board, read-only, and never another class’s items. A reading may carry up to five kinds of questions (multiple choice, multiple response, true/false, short answer, keyword); students take the quiz right on the page, the score is recorded, and the 🏅 points workbench turns the accumulated results into continuous-assessment evidence (V51).

Who it is for. Tutors author the content, learners work through it, parents watch the progress.

Why it matters. This is the actual teaching product. Everything a parent judges you on — scores, mastery, homework — originates from what is planned here.

How to use this page
  1. Set a reading in the ➕ card: title, author, subject, class (blank = everyone), the Drive/web link, optional instructions, due date, max score and attempt limit.
  2. Add comprehension questions (optional) — five kinds, marked automatically. A teacher may type the correct answer as text, a LETTER (A–D) or a NUMBER (1–4); all three mark correctly.
  3. Students open the reading from their shelf, press 📝 Take quiz, and their best attempt is recorded.
  4. At term’s end the 🏅 points workbench shows each learner’s accumulated points and pushes them into the scoresheet as continuous-assessment evidence.
  5. The table below remains the full staff catalogue — Export CSV / PDF for a printed version.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners and groups assigned to them, and nothing else.
  • Parent: Read-only — the class shelves and the shared shelf for their own children (V49).
  • Learner: Read-only — their own classes’ shelves and the shared shelf (V49).
