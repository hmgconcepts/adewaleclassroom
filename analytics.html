Analytics🔐 Owner / admin only

Studio-wide KPIs and trends, all live from the database: enrolment, income, attendance, quizzes, group sizes, staff mix, birthdays, demographics, an academic decision centre with term/session filters, and the top / at-risk learner tables. Nothing here is stored — every chart is computed from your tables on open.

Who it is for. Owners and admins making decisions; tutors see the teaching pages, not studio finances.

Why it matters. You cannot improve what you never measure. This page turns raw rows into the six decisions that matter: who to enroll next, which group is slipping, which subject needs a fix, who to celebrate, and whether the money is working.

How to use this page
  1. Read the KPI tiles for the headline position.
  2. Scan the trend charts — enrolment, fees, income vs expense.
  3. Open the Academic decision centre, pick a term/session/class/subject and press Analyse.
  4. Act on the Top 10 and At-risk tables; export either as JSON.
  5. Press 🔄 Refresh after entering new data anywhere in the studio.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access.
  • Admin: Full access.
  • Tutor: No access — this page includes studio finances.
  • Parent / Learner: No access.

🧭 Reading this page

  • Enrolment trend — new learners per month plus the cumulative total; a flat line means growth has stalled.
  • Attendance by engagement — green ≥ 85%, amber ≥ 70%, red below. Red bars are groups to call this week.
  • Income vs expense — the honest picture; income must clear expenses by enough to prepay tutor hours.
  • Academic decision centre — official scoresheet averages by class and subject, with pass rates.
  • At-risk table — the lowest averages. Intervene before the exam, not after.
