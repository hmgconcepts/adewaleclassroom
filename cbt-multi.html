Multi-subject CBT builder

Build one paper containing several subjects — exactly the UTME/JAMB pattern. Candidates get a single quiz code, see a tab per subject while sitting it, and questions never mix across subjects. When the paper is Graded, the database trigger writes one scoresheet row per subject plus an overall row, so Mathematics never smears into English.
