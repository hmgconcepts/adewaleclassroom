CBT results & audit

CBT results & audit 📊 Staff console

What this page is. Every quiz and exam this studio has ever set, each with a Results button beside it. Open one and you see who sat it — registered learners and anonymous candidates who used only a code — what they scored, how long they took, and any integrity flags raised while they worked. Open a single sitting and you see exactly what that candidate did: every question, the answer they gave, the answer expected, the mark awarded, and a timeline of anything the anti-cheat layer noticed.

Who it is for. Owner, admin and tutors. Families never reach this page; the database refuses it by role, not merely by hiding the link.

Why it matters. A score on its own tells you almost nothing. Item analysis tells you whether the class failed a question or the question failed the class — a facility below 25% usually means a wrong answer key, not thirty confused learners. Catching that before you publish a grade is the difference between a defensible mark and an argument with a parent.

How to use this page — step by step
  1. The table lists every paper with how many have sat it, how many are still unreviewed, and the class average. Anything needing attention is badged.
  2. Press 📊 Results beside a paper to open its results.
  3. Scan the summary strip: sat, average, highest, lowest, flagged, unreviewed.
  4. Press 🔍 Audit on any row to see that candidate's full paper, question by question.
  5. Use 🧪 Item analysis to find questions the whole class failed. Anything marked CHECK THE KEY should be verified before you release marks.
  6. Override a mark with ✏️ Review — useful for essays and code, which are marked by keyword and always need a human eye. Your comment is saved with the result.
  7. Notifications appear the moment anyone submits. The bell in the top bar and the panel below both show them; no email service is involved, so it costs nothing.
  8. Export any result set to CSV for a mark book or a parent meeting.
