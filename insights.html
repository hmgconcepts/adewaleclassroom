Insights Lab👪 Family view

A working notebook for the studio. The charts at the top show a learner's score trajectory and the flags the rule engine has raised. The desk below is where a hypothesis is written down BEFORE it is acted on — with the evidence, the proposed action, how you will know whether it worked, and eventually what actually happened. Requires sign-in. Parents see only their own children; a learner sees only themselves. Row Level Security enforces this in the database, not just in the interface.

Who it is for. Tutors and administrators. Notes are visible to their author, to the tutor of the learner or group concerned, and to administrators.

Why it matters. Teaching decisions are made on hunches all the time. Writing the hunch down with a measurable test is the difference between a studio that improves and one that merely has opinions. The outcome field is the point of the page.

How to use this page
  1. Give the note a title and say what it is about: a learner, a group, a subject or the studio as a whole.
  2. Write the observation and the evidence — numbers and dates, or it is an opinion.
  3. State how you will know it worked. This field is required on purpose.
  4. Move the status along as you go: open → testing → confirmed or rejected.
  5. Record the outcome. A rejected hypothesis with a written outcome is worth as much as a confirmed one.
What each role sees here
  • Owner: Full access across every learner.
  • Tutor: Full access for the learners assigned to them.
  • Parent: Read-only, and only for their own children.
  • Learner: Read-only, and only their own record.
